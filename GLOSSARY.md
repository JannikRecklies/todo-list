# Todo lists

This context describes named collections of todo items and the people who organize them.

## Language

**Todo list**:
A named collection of todos.
_Avoid_: List

**Todo**:
A plain-text item in a todo list. A todo can be completed; completed todos stay in their list.
_Avoid_: Task

**Completed todo**:
A todo marked as done. Its text stays visible, struck through.
_Avoid_: Done, finished
